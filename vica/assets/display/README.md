# VICA display previews

These PNG files are lossless web conversions of output from VICA's offline display
preview renderer. They were generated from representative canonical state and retain
the renderer's native proportions:

- `wind.png` — 256 × 256
- `incidents.png` — 160 × 64
- `minimum_corner_speed.png` — 160 × 64
- `relative_vertical.png` — 256 × 512

They are genuine display-element graphics, not a recreation or imagined placement.
They are also not live-session screenshots and do not validate VR placement, live
telemetry, comfort, or frame timing.

Regenerate them from `vica_display_preview.exe` when the corresponding renderer
changes. Do not redraw or composite them over fabricated simulator imagery.
